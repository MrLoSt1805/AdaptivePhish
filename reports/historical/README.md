# Invalidated temporal evidence

The three `*.invalidated.*` files are byte-preserved copies of the original
temporal report, governance decision and plot. They are **invalid evidence of
adaptive performance**. The original Phase 3 model was fitted on a random split;
1,342 of its fitting URLs also appear on or after the historical 2025-06-25
temporal cutoff. The old comparison must never drive promotion or conclusions.

The files at the original `reports/results/temporal_evaluation.json`,
`reports/results/promotion_decision.json` and
`reports/figures/temporal_f1_comparison.png` are also preserved, and equally
invalidated. The Phase 3 random-split experiment itself is not invalidated by
this later misuse of its model.

Use `reports/results/temporal_evaluation_v2.json` for the corrected partial-data
experiment. Its fitting, governance and final evaluation windows are disjoint.
